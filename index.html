His Story’s Server Rules

Please read the server rules before chatting.

1. Respect Everyone

Treat everyone with respect. Harassment, hate speech, bullying, or toxic behavior will not be tolerated. Disagreements must remain civil.

2. No Spam/Chat Flood

Do not send excessive or repetitive messages, emojis, reactions, links, or pings.

3. No NSFW

NSFW, explicit, gore, or inappropriate content of any kind is strictly prohibited.

4. No Advertising/Self-Promo

Promoting servers, social media, streams, or products requires staff approval. DM advertising is prohibited. Advertising within the server leads to a one-day mute, and DM advertising results in a ban.

5. No Channel Misuse

Stay on topic and use each channel only for its intended purpose to keep the server organized.

6. Avoid Drama

Personal arguments, call-outs, and unnecessary conflict should be kept out of the server.

7. Respect Privacy

Do not share personal or private information, yours or others', without clear consent.

8. No Impersonation

Avoid impersonating other members, staff, or public figures. Fictional or original characters are allowed, but do not mimic another person to mislead others. Parody names such as “His Story’s Son” are allowed when clearly not intended to mislead.

9. No Self-Harm Discussion

Discussions, encouragement, or jokes about self-harm or suicide are not allowed.

10. No Politics/Controversy

Political discussions, debates, or related content are generally not allowed. Civil and monitored conversations about politics or religion may be permitted at staff discretion. If discussion becomes hostile or creates a negative environment, participants may be punished regardless of who started it.

11. No Offensive Slurs

Offensive or derogatory slurs are strictly prohibited, including racial, homophobic, or discriminatory slurs, whether serious, joking, censored, or implied.

12. No Mass Mentions

Mass pinging someone without reasonable cause may result in mutes and possibly warnings depending on the extent of the behavior.

13. No Sexual Discussions

A few minor jokes are fine, but sexual discussions are not allowed because the majority of the server are minors.

14. Rule Enforcement & Loopholes

Do not attempt to bypass rules, punishments, or AutoMod. Violations may lead to warnings, mutes, kicks, or bans based on severity and recurrence. Loopholes and technicalities will still be moderated.

15. Be Positive

Help keep this server welcoming and enjoyable by engaging respectfully and constructively.

16. Act Normal

If a member is repeatedly being weird and/or cringe beyond just a few messages, a staff member may mute them for 5–10 minutes.

17. Follow Discord TOS

Any content or activity that violates Discord's Terms of Service, including illegal content, cheats, hacks, or piracy, is forbidden.

Additional Rules & Information

Staff get final say in situations. If you believe something is unjustified, report it to a head moderator or administrator.

We follow Discord’s Terms of Service. Review Discord’s Terms of Service.

Do not ping His/Her Story. They are very busy people; doing so will get you muted.
